# Product

## Register

product

## Users

A single general desktop user browsing personal image folders directly from the local filesystem.

## Product Purpose

Provide the shortest possible path from selecting a filesystem folder to seeing its images. Success means navigation feels immediate and the image grid stays visually dominant.

## Brand Personality

Warm, quiet, photo-focused.

## Anti-references

Avoid enterprise dashboards, photo-editing controls, decorative interface chrome, metadata panels, filenames, captions, and playful consumer styling.

## Design Principles

- Let photographs occupy nearly all available gallery space.
- Keep filesystem navigation familiar and secondary.
- Remove labels and controls that do not help folder navigation.
- Prefer immediate, predictable behavior over customization.
- Treat empty, loading, and unavailable folders quietly and clearly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal accessibility target is required for this personal tool. Preserve baseline keyboard interaction, visible focus, semantic controls, and reduced-motion behavior where inexpensive.
